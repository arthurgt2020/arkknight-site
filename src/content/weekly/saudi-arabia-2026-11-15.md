Weekly executive threat intelligence for Saudi Arabia, covering 9 to 15 November 2026. Threat level: **ELEVATED**. The full 29-page report was published on LinkedIn as a document post.

## Five things executives should know

1. Extortion groups keep naming Saudi firms on leak sites. None of the named organisations has confirmed a breach.
2. N0n is working through Gulf retail and ports, posting loyalty-programme data and a list of shipping firms. One Gulf retail group confirmed its loyalty system was accessed.
3. France blamed an Iranian Revolutionary Guard unit for sabotaging refrigeration controllers in about 700 shops. The unit holds tools for Schneider Electric, Siemens and Rockwell industrial equipment.
4. Firewall and email-gateway flaws are under attack. CISA listed exploited Barracuda and Check Point flaws on 10 and 13 November; Saudi CERT had issued no alert for either by 17 November.
5. Missile and drone attacks continued against energy infrastructure. No confirmed cyberattack on a Saudi organisation was found this week.

**Bottom line:** at least two more Saudi organisations will appear on leak sites by 17 December (likely, 65 to 75%).

**What to watch:** Saudi CERT alerts on the Check Point and Barracuda flaws, any victim confirmation, and the Arab cybersecurity ministers' meeting in Doha.

Every claim in the report is sourced and was checked twice: 102 sources, each Admiralty-rated.
